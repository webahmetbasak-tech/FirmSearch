# 2026 Official-source research

Research date: **2026-09-27**. The implementation uses stable releases only.

## Angular

- Angular's [release schedule](https://angular.dev/reference/releases) lists v22 as actively supported. The installed production toolchain is Angular core/CLI/SSR/build **22.2.0**.
- Angular's [server and hybrid rendering guide](https://angular.dev/best-practices/performance/ssr) defines `RenderMode.Server`, `RenderMode.Prerender`, and `RenderMode.Client`. This project prerenders stable public overview/legal pages and server-renders dynamic entity/404 routes.
- Hydration uses Angular's official `provideClientHydration()`. Critical headings, entities, metadata, links and JSON-LD are present in the response HTML; indexing does not depend on browser JavaScript.

## Google Search

- [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): people-first content, crawlable links and technically accessible content remain the baseline.
- [JavaScript SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics): rendered HTML must expose visible content; structured data may be generated by JavaScript but must be testable. SSR is used here to reduce rendering dependency.
- [Canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls): redirects and `rel=canonical` are strong consolidation signals; sitemap inclusion is weaker. Orthographic aliases therefore return HTTP 301 to one canonical route.
- [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): doorway pages and scaled content made mainly for ranking are prohibited. Special-intent pages stay absent until they have distinct inventory and value.
- [AI features and websites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) and the [generative AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): there is no additional AI schema, AI meta tag, or machine-readable AI file required for AI Overviews/AI Mode. Standard indexing eligibility and useful content apply.
- [LocalBusiness structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business): Google requires real business data; ratings/reviews must follow review rules. The platform does not emit fake addresses or ratings.
- Google's [vehicle listing announcement](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/10/vehicle-listings-structured-data) and [onboarding guide](https://developers.google.com/vehicle-listings/onboarding-guide) explicitly address **for-sale dealership inventory**, not rental fleets. Rental vehicles are not marked up for that rich result.
- [Sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap): a sitemap is a hint and should contain canonical URLs. Demo/noindex entities are excluded.

## Schema.org

- [AutoRental](https://schema.org/AutoRental) is the specific local-business subtype used for a genuine rental company.
- [Car](https://schema.org/Car) / `Vehicle` represent the vehicle entity. `Offer` is emitted only if a visible real price exists; rental semantics use the GoodRelations `LeaseOut` business function.
- `Person`, `Service`, `BreadcrumbList`, `CollectionPage`, `ItemList`, `WebSite` and `LocalBusiness` are limited to properties supported by the actual visible record.
- Schema.org validity is not represented as Google rich-result eligibility.

## AI and search crawlers

- OpenAI's [crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) and [publisher FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq) distinguish OAI-SearchBot/ChatGPT-User from GPTBot. Search/user access is allowed; training access is denied by current project policy.
- Anthropic's [official crawler guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler) distinguishes Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User and training-oriented ClaudeBot. Search/user access is allowed; ClaudeBot is denied.
- Perplexity's [official bot guide](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots) distinguishes PerplexityBot from user-requested fetching. Both are allowed for public content; private conversion paths remain blocked.
- [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a), [IndexNow](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/indexnow-0z209wby), and [AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c) support canonical URLs, accurate freshness, change-only IndexNow submissions and post-launch citation monitoring.

## Decisions

1. Angular 22.2 official SSR/hybrid rendering; no beta/RC.
2. No indexable demo inventory. Empty verticals and demo detail pages return `noindex,follow`.
3. No FAQ rich-result strategy, special AI markup, hidden AI content, or `llms.txt` ranking claim. `llms.txt` is a low-cost informational aid only.
4. No Google vehicle-listing markup for rental inventory.
5. Search crawlers are distinct from training crawlers in `robots.txt`.
6. Production origin, real contacts, consent/privacy details and provider inventory must be supplied before launch.
